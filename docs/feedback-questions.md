# SNM Feedback Forms — Complete Question List

This document lists every question asked in both online feedback forms, along with all the answer options.

---

## 1) Feedback for Branch Incharge

### Personal Details

| # | Question | Type | Options / Format |
|---|----------|------|------------------|
| 1 | Your Name | Text | Free text |
| 2 | Your Phone Number | Number | 10-digit mobile number |
| 3 | Type of Samagam held at your branch | Dropdown (single select) | EMS, Bal, Mahila |
| 4 | Pracharak Mahatma Name who visited your sector | Text | Free text |
| 5 | Your Zone Number | Search | Search by zone number or area name |
| 6 | Your Sector | Dropdown | Depends on the selected zone (shown for special zones only) |
| 7 | Area Name | Text (auto-filled) | Filled automatically from zone data |

### Feedback on the Samagam

| # | Question | Type | Options / Format |
|---|----------|------|------------------|
| 8 | Arrival Time of Pracharak Saint | Radio (single select) | On time · Late |
| 9 | (If Late) At what time did the Pracharak Saint arrive? | Time | Time picker |
| 10 | Content of the Pracharak Saint *(question changes with the Samagam type selected)* | See below | See below |
| 11 | How was the conduct of Pracharak Saint? | Radio (single select) | Excellent · Very Good · Good · Average · Poor |
| 12 | Other than this, is there any scope of improvements needed for the sewa performed by Pracharak Saint? | Textarea | Free text |

**Category-specific content question (No. 10):**
- **EMS**: What was the ratio of the Vichar (discourse) done in English and Hindi? — Radio: 70:30 English: Hindi (preferred) · 60:40 · 50:50 · 40:60 (more Hindi)
- **Bal**: Did the Pracharak Saint use child-friendly (Bal) examples and analogies to help the kids understand the discourse? — Radio: Yes · No
- **Mahila**: Did the Pracharak Saint use the broader aspects of the women's lives and relevant examples/instances of women to help them connect with the discourse? How relevant did it feel? — Radio: Yes · No
